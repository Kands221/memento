## Why Memento benefits from running AI locally

**Video line (8 s):** "Your journal holds your most private words. On-device AI means no servers, no account, it works offline, and it's free to run. Your words stay yours."

**Written answer (for the submission form):**
> Memento is a journal, the most private writing people do, so on-device AI isn't a feature here; it's the foundation.
> 1. **Private by design, not by policy.** Entries, voice and conversations with Sol never leave the iPhone. There's no server or account to breach, so people can be fully honest, which is the point of a journal.
> 2. **Personal without the risk.** Sol can search the writer's whole journal to remember what helped before, because that index lives only on the phone. A cloud product would have to upload someone's inner life to do this.
> 3. **Always there.** Tagging, Sol, voice and painting work offline, at 2 a.m., on a plane, with no outages and no waiting on a network.
> 4. **Free to run.** There's no per-message cloud cost, so the AI that helps people reflect doesn't have to sit behind a paywall.

## Before you record (15 minutes)

- [ ] Apple Intelligence is on, and **You → On-device AI** says **READY · WORKS OFFLINE**.
- [ ] **You → Demo → Clear journal**, then **Load sample journal** (it includes the walk with Priya that Sol recalls).
- [ ] **Sol's voice:** download an Enhanced or Premium voice (Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English), then pick it under **You → Sol's voice**. Turn Sol's speaker on.
- [ ] Warm up the model once: write "test", save, wait for tags, then delete the entry. Ask Sol one throwaway question, then close Sol.
- [ ] Turn on **airplane mode**, Do Not Disturb and silent mode. Set battery above 50%, turn off Low Power Mode, and set brightness to max.
- [ ] Rehearse the spoken line to Sol twice. Short and clear transcribes best.

## Recording and editing

- **Capture:** use the iPhone's own screen recording with the microphone on, so Sol's voice and yours are captured. Do 2–3 takes of each section rather than one long take.
- **Edit:**
  - **Jump-cut every wait** ("Finding details…", Sol thinking) down to about 0.5 s. Never speed up Sol's voice.
  - **Formats:** export 9:16 at 1080×1920 for social, and 16:9 with the phone in a frame for judges.
  - **Captions:** burned-in, large and high-contrast, at most 6 words each.
  - **Music:** soft and low, ducked under VO and Sol.
- **Honesty checks:**
  - Keep the "suggestions, not diagnosis" tone.
  - Don't show anything the app can't do live.
  - If you record in the simulator, don't show airplane mode.
- **Final length:** 55–58 s. Check it once with the sound off; the captions alone should tell the story.
