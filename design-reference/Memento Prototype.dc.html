MEMENTO · IPHONE PROTOTYPE

Write naturally. Notice gently. Find it again.

The journal leads; on-device tagging quietly connects it. Sol and summaries sit one level down, reached from the things you wrote.

Navigation · five tabs
JournalToday, streak, recent entries. Unreviewed suggestions are marked, never hidden. DiscoverEvery kept tag, grouped as feelings, situations, what helped, topics. Leads to tag detail and summaries. Write +One sheet for every mode. Sol lives here, below the writing modes, as an option. NotebooksCollections with search, tag filters and moving entries. YouReminders and streak, summaries, Sol, on-device AI, appearance.
The core journey
1
Write. Saving is instant and never waits for AI. Switching modes keeps your words.
2
Notice. On this iPhone, Memento suggests details, each linked to the words behind it. Dashed = suggested, filled = kept. Keep, edit, remove, undo, or leave for later.
3
Find again. A kept tag opens every related moment across notebooks, then lets you select them for a summary.
Jump to a screen

Try: Write → Free → “Use example” → Save. Tweaks switch AI states (needs download, interrupted, failing, unsupported), dark mode, larger text and reduced motion.
